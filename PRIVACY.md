# Peelpad privacy policy

*Last updated: 23 October 2026*

Peelpad is a sticky-notes app for Windows made by Niro54321. It has no account,
no ads and no analytics.

## What stays on your PC

Everything you write in Peelpad (your notes, tasks, history and settings) and
any pictures you upload as skins are stored only on your own computer, in
Peelpad's data folder (`%APPDATA%\Peelpad`, or the app's private folder when
installed from the Microsoft Store). Peelpad never sends them anywhere.
Uninstalling Peelpad (Store version) or deleting that folder removes them.

## The one thing that goes online: the Weather look

If you choose the **Weather** look and type a city, Peelpad sends that city
name to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) to find it, then sends its map
coordinates to Open-Meteo every 20 minutes to get the current weather. It does
this only while the Weather look is in use. Nothing else is sent: not your notes,
not your name, not any identifier. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy is at
https://open-meteo.com/en/terms.

If you never use the Weather look, Peelpad does not connect to the internet.

## Children

Peelpad does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
