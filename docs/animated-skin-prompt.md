# Prompt for an animated Peelpad skin

Paste this into an AI image or animation tool, or send it to an artist. Change the animation idea to your own.

```text
Make a looping animated sticker for a sticky-note app (Peelpad).

Format
- Square canvas, 600 x 600 px.
- Transparent background (alpha), saved as an animated GIF, APNG or animated WebP.
  Animated WebP or APNG look best; GIF has only 256 colours and hard edges.
- 2 to 4 seconds long, 12 to 24 frames per second, looping seamlessly
  (the last frame flows straight into the first).
- Under 8 MB, at most 150 frames.

Layout
- The middle is a calm, plain card where a to-do list will be written:
  480 x 444 px, starting 60 px from the left, right and bottom and 96 px from
  the top. Keep this area one flat light colour (or one flat dark colour),
  with no motion, no patterns and no text.
- All movement happens around the card: on the frame, in the corners, along
  the bottom edge or peeking over the top edge.
- The outside shape can be anything (round, wavy, a character holding the card);
  everything outside that shape must be fully transparent, not a painted
  checkerboard.

Style
- Cute, bold, sticker-like: thick dark outlines, flat colours, soft blush.
- Readable at small size (the note is often only 250 to 350 px wide).

Animation idea (change this part)
- A small chibi skull with tiny legs runs along the bottom edge from right to
  left, disappears on the left and comes back in from the right.
- A bat flaps its wings in the top-right corner, and a few stars twinkle on the frame.

Other ideas to swap in:
- A black cat walks along the top edge, its tail swaying.
- A ghost floats up and down beside the card, blinking.
- Little hearts rise from the bottom corners and fade.
- A spider lowers itself on a thread from the top edge and climbs back up.
- Rain drops slide down a dark frame while a tiny umbrella sways.
```
